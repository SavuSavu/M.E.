A QUICK TOUR

A new version in a few steps.

  1. Bring in a model. Drop an STL or STEP file, or try an example. STL coordinates are treated as millimetres.
  2. Look around. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. F fits the model in view.
  3. Finish an edge. Click CAD edges or select them from Browse edges. Set a fillet radius or chamfer distance and Apply. Tangent following extends selection along connected curves.
  4. Edit an STL. Scale, rotate, smooth, or sculpt. Refine adds vertices for the brush. Closed meshes under 20,000 triangles can attempt solid conversion; curved STL surfaces remain faceted.
  5. Keep the next version. Export STL for your slicer, or Save project to keep CAD geometry. Undo reverses edits; a new import starts a new history.

Save before importing another model or closing the tab. Mesh checks count open, non-manifold, and degenerate edges/faces; they do not check self-intersections or guarantee printability.
